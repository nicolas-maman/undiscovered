Forward test

The first test looked back at 2018 to 2023, and the people who ran it knew roughly what had happened. This one ranks pairs of fields now, and is judged on citations that have not been written yet.

What was ranked

26,475 pairs of research topics from different domains (physical, life, health and social sciences) that had at most one citing work between them up to the end of 2025, drawn from the first test's 300 topics.

By what

The first test's network model, unchanged: it reads only the shape of the citation network around each pair, not what the topics are about. A ranking by the topics' size and growth is kept as the comparison.

Locked in

The ranking, both models and the records they were computed from are published with checksums, timestamped in Bitcoin block 971017 (01:11 UTC, 12 October 2026). Anyone can check that they have not changed since, without trusting this project or GitHub.

How it is judged

A pair counts as connected if at least 3 works published from 2026 to 2031 cite across it. Counts are made each year from 2027 and labelled interim; the result is read in mid 2032. The test passes if the network ranking beats the size and growth ranking, with the 95% interval of the difference above zero.
